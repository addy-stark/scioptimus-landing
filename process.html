How SciOptimus Works in Your Lab

Step by step, from scattered data to trusted operations and audit-ready outcomes.

5-Step Implementation

1

Capture Inventory Inputs

Import current spreadsheets and label each reagent, batch, storage location, owner, and expiration.

2

Data Collection for Insurance & Trust Infrastructure

Build a trusted record of chemical value, handling history, custody, usage events, and risk class. This gives insurers and compliance teams consistent, verified operating evidence.

3

Automate Controls

Set reorder thresholds, expiry alerts, check-in/check-out flows, and equipment utilization logs.

4

Connect Notes + Operations

Link ELN entries with inventory and equipment records, so experiments and materials stay synchronized.

5

Produce Audit-Ready Outputs

Generate timestamped, searchable, exportable logs for internal audits, grant reporting, and external inspections.

Deployment Reality

SciOptimus is the lab operating system for teams that have outgrown spreadsheets but do not need costly, months-long enterprise rollouts. Most teams can start with core inventory and compliance workflows within days.
